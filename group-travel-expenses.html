Free group expense tracking for backpackers

Keep hostel bills, shared taxis, meals and activities in one trip ledger. Review who paid, how the cost was split, and where the group spent its budget.

Open Trip Finance

Set up a trip before you leave

  1. Create a trip, select a currency, and invite your travel companions.
  2. Record each shared cost with its date, category, payer and split.
  3. Open Trip tools to search expenses, check the spending report, or attach a receipt.

Use the same split for recurring costs

If two travellers share one room and a third has a smaller share, the trip owner can save weights such as 2, 2 and 1. Select Use saved split on each new expense that should use those proportions. Choose another method for costs that need a different split.

Keep receipts and item details together

Attach a receipt image, then add lines for things like room charges, breakfast or parking. You can label a line with a traveller’s name and compare the item total to the expense. Record the actual split on the expense itself.

Plan for travel across India or abroad

For a hostel trip through India, record shared costs in INR. For an international itinerary, agree on a trip currency and the conversion rate your group wants to use before entering expenses paid in another currency. Keep a note of the original amount when it helps explain a charge.
